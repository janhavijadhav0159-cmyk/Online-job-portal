About Our Job Portal

JobPortal is an online platform designed to connect job seekers and employers.

Job seekers can create profiles, search for suitable jobs, upload resumes and apply for available positions.

Employers can create company profiles, post job vacancies and manage applications from candidates.

The main goal of JobPortal is to make the job searching and recruitment process simple and convenient.
